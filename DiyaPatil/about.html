Our Heritage

Founded by master perfumers in 2026, Aura Fragrances was established with a singular, passionate vision: to restore true artistry, emotion, and craftsmanship to modern perfumery. We believe that a scent is an invisible, deeply personal signature that leaves a lasting impression.

Our Mission & Ethical Promise

We are dedicated to delivering unparalleled luxury without compromising our rigorous ethical standards. Our core philosophies include:

  • Ethical Sourcing: We travel the globe to partner directly with farmers who grow our botanicals sustainably.
  • 100% Cruelty-Free: We have never, and will never, test our formulas on animals.
  • Small-Batch Craftsmanship: Every bottle is hand-blended, macerated, and aged to perfection in our boutique laboratory.
